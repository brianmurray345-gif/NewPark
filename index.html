Our Mission

TypingPark is on a mission to make keyboarding accessible to every learner. We believe that typing should be a skill anyone can build confidently, without cost barriers or complicated tools. That’s why we provide free, educational typing games designed specifically for students.

As classrooms embrace more technology, students need self-paced digital activities that challenge them, motivate them, and grow with them. At TypingPark, we fully believe in that future. Our platform supports it by offering a collection of targeted typing games—each one crafted to strengthen a specific aspect of keyboarding, from accuracy and rhythm to speed and hand positioning.

Every game adapts to each student’s skill level, ensuring the experience stays engaging and appropriately challenging. Over time, this adaptive practice helps learners steadily increase their typing speed (WPM) and build long-term confidence at the keyboard.

Whether students are just getting started or looking to refine their skills, TypingPark is here to support their journey—one keystroke at a time.
