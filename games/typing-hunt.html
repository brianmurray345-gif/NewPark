Typing Hunt

A free educational typing game for students.

  • Free
  • Plays in your browser
  • Educational

The game loads right here (about 30 MB).

Typing Hunt needs a keyboard — it works best on a computer.

How to play

  1. Press Play and wait for the game to load.
  2. Click inside the game so it can hear your keyboard.
  3. Keep your fingers on the home row! Press Fullscreen for a bigger view, and Esc to leave it.
